CASE FACT SUMMARY
=================
CASE NUMBER: LAB-2023-01122  
PARTIES: Sneha Kulkarni, Former Senior Engineer; TechNova Systems Pvt. Ltd.  
KEY DATES: Employment from March 2019 to August 2023; Termination on August 30, 2023; Filing date September 5, 2023.

CLAIMS: Wrongful termination; Non-payment of dues; Violation of employment contract Clause 11.

EVIDENCE SOURCES: Employment contract, termination notice, payment records.
